◆ Our Business

What we do

Ocean Technology and Energy Corporation is an Australian-based commodity trading company specializing in the export of coal and iron ore. We connect Australian mining operations with international markets across Asia and Europe.

Our focus is on reliable sourcing, quality assurance, and building long-term relationships with both suppliers and buyers. We understand the complexities of international commodity trading and provide seamless solutions for our partners.
